Innovation Options LLC was founded by [David Binetti](https://www.dbinetti.com), a six-time entrepreneur and creator of the Innovation Options Framework.  We write options as would an investment firm, develop them, and provide all option buyers with the final decision on whether to exercise.  These buyers own all IP from start to finish.

David received his Bachelors with Highest Honors and University Distinction from UC Berkeley and his MBA with Top Thesis honors from UCLA.  He is considered one of the pioneers of the [Lean Startup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lean_startup) movement, with a specialization in Innovation Accounting.
